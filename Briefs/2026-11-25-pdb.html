Zelensky Confirms Sparta II Strike as EU Proposes 22nd Sanctions Package

Zelensky confirmed that Ukraine hit the sanctioned cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland on 21 Nov. The European Commission proposed a 22nd sanctions package as Moscow rejected an EU role in talks.

Change Since Last Report

The 24 Nov report carried the Libya sinking and Finland's assessment. New: Zelensky's confirmation, Ukraine's Article 51 position, the EU package, Peskov's statement and the Patriot delay.

Key Facts

  • Zelensky confirmed the drone strike in a Bloomberg interview; the ship drifted into Finnish waters near Loviisa and no injuries were reported (Kyiv Independent).
  • Ukraine's Foreign Ministry called the 22 Nov strikes off Benghazi lawful self-defense under Article 51 of the UN Charter (Kyiv Independent).
  • Russia towed the Sparta II to St. Petersburg (TASS).
  • The proposed package targets more than 70 shadow-fleet vessels and about 60 banks, and needs unanimity (Epoch Times).
  • The Financial Times reports Defense Secretary Hegseth has not approved about 60 Patriot interceptors allocated to Ukraine; Zelensky says shipments are delayed (Daily Maverick, via Reuters).

Points of Dispute

Legitimacy of the Sparta II strike. Zelensky says the strike was legitimate because the ship carried weapons, and concedes it may worry allies. Finland's defense minister warned Kyiv about risks to civilian shipping in the narrow strait; Prime Minister Orpo said Ukraine has the right to defend itself (Kyiv Independent).

EU role in talks. European governments seek a four-party format. Peskov said on 24 Nov that Russia sees no added value in EU participation and has not agreed to a broader format (Epoch Times).

Watch

  2. Does Russia carry out a stated retaliation, or does Libya's eastern government seek a UN Security Council meeting, by 05 Dec? Assessment: Roughly even chance (low confidence), based on Moscow's pledge to respond and no retaliation reported.
  3. Does the Pentagon approve release of the roughly 60 allocated Patriot interceptors by 15 Dec? Assessment: Roughly even chance (low confidence), based on one FT report relayed by Reuters and no Pentagon statement.

Markets Price December Fed Hike at 96 Percent as Stocks Slip on China Tariff Reports

Traders price a 25-basis-point Fed hike at the 08–09 Dec meeting at 96 percent, and stocks slipped early Tuesday after Monday's rebound. The Federal Reserve has issued no press release since 20 Nov (Federal Reserve).

Change Since Last Report

The 24 Nov report carried Monday's rebound. Tuesday's early session gave back part of it, led by Nvidia.

Key Facts

  • CME FedWatch shows 96 percent of traders expect a 25-basis-point hike in December, to 4 percent–4.25 percent (HousingWire).
  • Early Tuesday the Nasdaq was down 1.3 percent, the S&P 500 down 0.6 percent and the Dow up 0.4 percent (Al Jazeera).
  • Nvidia fell more than 5 percent on reports of new China tariffs, after a 31 percent jump on Monday (Al Jazeera).
  • The Conference Board's consumer confidence index, a private series, rose to 103.3 in November from 91.9 (The Hill, headline and lede only).

Points of Dispute

Whether the Fed will follow through. Market pricing puts a December hike at 96 percent. Dan Ribler of Longbridge says the Fed talks tough but has not acted: “Inflation has been above 2% year over year 57 months in a row” (HousingWire). The 09 Dec decision settles it.

Watch

  2. Does the S&P 500 close below its 20 Nov level of 6,910 on any day through today? Assessment: Very unlikely (low confidence), based on Monday's 4.7 percent rise and a 0.6 percent early decline Tuesday.
