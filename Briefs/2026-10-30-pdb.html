BEA Q3 2026 GDP advance estimate released 29 Oct (3.4% vs. ~2.8% consensus) — the primary new development driving this topic. December FOMC hike probability moved to 85% from 81%. Freddie Mac PMMS showed slight easing to 7.24% from 7.32%.

Key Facts

  • December 9 FOMC rate hike probability: 85% per federal funds futures markets as of 29 Oct close — up four percentage points from the 81% consensus immediately following the 28 Oct unanimous-hold decision (corrected for subsequent disclosure of two FOMC dissents favoring an immediate hike).

Both sides cite different Federal Reserve governor signals regarding the December decision.

Cathedral/Anthropic Breach Day 5: AI Cyber Defense Act Introduced in House ↑ Trending

The Cathedral/Anthropic AI infrastructure breach entered its fifth day; Reps. Sarah Chen (D-CA) and Marcus Webb (R-TX) introduced the AI Cyber Defense Act (HB 10873), citing the breach in floor statements — the first major federal AI security legislation to receive bipartisan co-sponsorship. The bill mandates security audits, 72-hour breach notification, and establishes an AI Cyber Defense Office within CISA. PIR 4.3 story date reset this edition.

Change Since Last Report

HB 10873 (AI Cyber Defense Act) introduced 29 Oct — bipartisan, first of its kind (new this edition). Federal cyber drill entered Day 4. Anthropic issued second public statement acknowledging "an active investigation." No official breach attribution confirmed. PIR 4.3 stale alert resolved: story date reset to 30 Oct 2026.

Key Facts

  • HB 10873 (AI Cyber Defense Act) introduced 29 Oct by Chen (D-CA) and Webb (R-TX); provisions include mandatory federal AI system security audits, 72-hour breach notification requirements, and establishment of an AI Cyber Defense Office within CISA. Sponsors cited the Cathedral/Anthropic breach in floor statements.
  • Anthropic issued a second public statement acknowledging "an active investigation" into the breach and declining to confirm specifics; the company stated no customer data was accessed. No official government attribution for the breach has been confirmed.
  • Federal governmentwide cyber drill entered its fourth day; three or more federal AI procurements remain on hold pending security review. DOGE-related mixed court rulings this period — some workforce protections reinstated, others denied — continue to affect federal AI oversight capacity (W4.4 — fifth consecutive designation recommendation).

Points of Dispute

Technology industry groups have expressed concern that HB 10873's 72-hour breach notification requirement is operationally unworkable for AI systems, where attribution and scope determination can take significantly longer. Security researchers are divided on whether the bill's AI Cyber Defense Office model appropriately separates AI security from traditional cybersecurity governance. Some conservatives argue the bill grants excessive regulatory authority to CISA without corresponding congressional accountability mechanisms.
