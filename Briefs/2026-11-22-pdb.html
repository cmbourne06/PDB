Iran Fires on Bahrain as Qatari Mediators Visit Tehran

Bahrain says it intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks late on 21 Nov, and the Revolutionary Guard says it targeted U.S. positions at the al-Riffa and Awali bases. A Qatari delegation held two hours of talks in Tehran on Friday night.

Change Since Last Report

The 21 Nov report covered Trump saying Iran wants a deal he will not accept. New: a Revolutionary Guard claim against Bahrain, after earlier claims on 13 and 16 Nov, and Qatari mediators in Tehran.

Key Facts

  • Bahrain’s Interior Ministry reports material damage at an industrial facility and no injuries. The Guard says it destroyed two Patriot radars (Al Jazeera).
  • Iran’s deputy foreign minister said the Qatari talks were not yet formal negotiations (Al Jazeera).
  • Foreign Minister Araghchi says reopening Hormuz is “not difficult” if the U.S. accepts conditions including an end to the war on all fronts and lifting the U.S. blockade (Al Jazeera).
  • Al Arabiya, via TASS, cites an unnamed source: enriched uranium is the main obstacle; the U.S. wants the stockpile liquidated, and Iran says it must stay in the country (TASS).

Damage. The Revolutionary Guard claims two Patriot radars destroyed. Bahrain reports only an industrial-facility fire. IRNA, citing Bahrain News, says a U.S. Fifth Fleet interceptor crashed on Sitra Island after a Patriot malfunction. A CENTCOM or Pentagon statement: not available (Al Jazeera).

State of talks. Iran says it remains in contact with mediators. Al Arabiya reports a deadlock over uranium (TASS).

Executive Order Requires Contractors to Notify White House of Congressional Requests

Executive Order 14438, dated 20 Nov, requires federal contractors to notify the White House and the contracting agency of congressional requests about their work with the executive branch. It arrives before the Democratic-majority Congress takes office in January.

Key Facts

  • Section 2 requires contractors to notify the White House and the agency “immediately” of any pending or future congressional request about their executive-branch communications or work (EO 14438).
  • Contractors must follow White House or agency instructions to withhold communications deemed protected by executive privilege; the order extends to subcontractors (EO 14438).
  • Section 4 directs agencies to “consider” termination, debarment or suspension for noncompliance; Section 6 says the order creates no enforceable rights (EO 14438).
  • OMB is to issue guidance. The Epoch Times reports Rep. Robert Garcia, expected to chair House Oversight, has pledged investigations of Trump, his family, DHS/ICE and the Epstein files (Epoch Times).

Points of Dispute

Purpose of the order. The White House says some congressional leaders threatened to harass contractors and that privilege must be asserted first. The Washington Examiner describes the order as protection against “weaponization” by the incoming majority; that is the Examiner’s framing, not the order’s. A Democratic response: not available (Washington Examiner).

Watch

  1. Will the Democratic-majority Congress challenge executive actions, including EO 14438, in January? Assessment: likely (moderate confidence), based on the incoming majority’s stated oversight plans.
  2. Does any member of Congress challenge EO 14438 by letter, hearing notice, legislation or suit by 31 Jan 2027? Assessment: likely (low confidence), based on Garcia’s pledged investigations; no Democratic response has been reported.

Reports Say FBI National Crime Database Was Breached; FBI Has Not Confirmed

CNN and NBC reported on 20 Nov, citing two people briefed, that the FBI’s National Crime Information Center (NCIC) was compromised. The FBI has not confirmed a breach, and Trump appeared to confirm the hacking in a television interview.

Key Facts

  • NCIC tracks fugitives and registered sex offenders and holds warrants. Fox News, citing three sources, says it is unclear what data was taken (Al Jazeera; Daily Caller).
  • Al Jazeera calls it the bureau’s second hack this year; Daily Caller cites a 22 Sep ShinyHunters attack on its job portal, with an arrest on 09 Oct (Daily Caller).
  • Trump said in a Saturday interview, “I will say that it’s not good. I don’t like that” (Al Jazeera); he also said he tries to take a positive view (Washington Examiner).

Points of Dispute

Scope and responsibility. CNN and NBC, via Al Jazeera, cite anonymous sources that NCIC was compromised. The FBI did not immediately comment. The data taken and who is responsible: not available (Al Jazeera).

Trump’s remarks. The Washington Examiner paraphrases Trump as saying the hack might reveal voter fraud and a “Ukraine hoax.” Al Jazeera and Daily Caller quote him without that remark (Washington Examiner).

Watch

  1. Do the FBI or the Justice Department publicly confirm the NCIC breach and its scope by 05 Dec? Assessment: likely (low confidence), based on Trump’s remarks and multi-outlet reporting; the FBI has not commented.
