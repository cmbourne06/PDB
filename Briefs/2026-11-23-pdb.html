Iran’s navies say two tankers on unauthorized routes hit mines or caught fire near Hormuz on 22 Nov; no shipping authority or CENTCOM account is available. Trump told Fox he is inclined to order new strikes after the new year if there is no deal, and the blockade stays.

Change Since Last Report

The 20 Nov edition reported Trump holding off on major strikes until the new year. New: he now says he is inclined to strike after 01 Jan without a deal, and Iran claims two tanker explosions.

Key Facts

  • The Revolutionary Guard navy says two supertankers on “unauthorized” routes exploded after hitting mines north of Al Ghanam Island; the army navy says they caught fire (Xinhua).
  • Iranian state media say the army navy found the tankers inside a restricted area with tracking off (Al Jazeera).
  • Trump called the negotiators stalling and said, “They’re more aggressive now than before the election” (Fox News).

Tanker fate. The Guard navy says mines; the army navy says fire. No shipping authority or CENTCOM account confirms either, and whether these are the two Khasab incidents reported on 22 Nov: not available (Xinhua).

Talks. Trump says Iran is stalling. Iran says it stays in contact with Qatari mediators (Al Jazeera).

Fifth Circuit Rules House Passed 2023 Omnibus Unconstitutionally Through Proxy Votes

The Fifth Circuit ruled 9–8 on 20 Nov that the House unconstitutionally passed the roughly $1.7 trillion Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023, because proxy presence cannot form a quorum. The remedy blocks enforcement of the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act against Texas only.

Key Facts

  • The court held that members voting by proxy cannot count toward the quorum the Constitution requires; Judge Wilson wrote, “Presence-by-proxy does not suffice” (Epoch Times).
  • The article says the ruling does not undo the 2023 law; it blocks enforcement of the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act against Texas (Epoch Times).
  • Judge Graves, dissenting, wrote that the Constitution does not expressly require physical presence (Epoch Times).
  • The Hill’s headline says the ruling found the $1.7 trillion funding bill unconstitutional (The Hill).

Points of Dispute

Reach of the ruling. The Epoch Times reports the injunction covers Texas only. The Washington Examiner’s headline says a court overturned the federal pregnant workers’ law. A Better Balance’s president says the reasoning could reach protections across the Fifth Circuit (Washington Examiner). The opinion text: not available.

Watch

  1. Does the Justice Department, the House or Texas seek rehearing or Supreme Court review of the Fifth Circuit’s proxy-voting ruling by 31 Dec? Assessment: roughly even chance (low confidence), based on a 9–8 split and a remedy limited to Texas.

Key Facts

  • FlightAware showed over 1,760 Delta flights delayed and about 45 canceled; Delta says about 70 percent of delays came from the automation issue (Epoch Times).

Cause of the outages. Clayton attributes them to system upgrades; that is his assertion, and no agency finding has been published. CISA, per Mullin, sees no cyberattack.
