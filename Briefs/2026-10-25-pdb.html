BLUF

The ruling is the first major legal check on DOGE's access to federal data systems and sets a precedent for challenges to other agency data-sharing arrangements.

Exchange4media, SecurityToday, and others report new incidents in this collection window.

On Black October attribution: security researchers dispute whether breaches constitute truly "autonomous" AI agents or human actors leveraging AI tools — a distinction with significant implications for both legal liability and defensive strategy.
