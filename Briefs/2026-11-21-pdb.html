Russian Missiles Fall in Poland During Mass Strike on Ukraine

Poland says two Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone fell on its territory during Russia’s overnight attack on Ukraine on 20 Nov, and it asked NATO for more aircraft. The attack killed at least nine people in Ukraine.

Change Since Last Report

The 20 Nov I&W item reported Putin’s promise of a harsher response to the Crimean Bridge strikes. New: a mass strike followed, and debris from it reached NATO territory.

Key Facts

  • Ukraine’s air force counted 84 cruise missiles and 404 drones, and Zelensky added 27 ballistic missiles; three Kyiv bridges were damaged and a 750-kV substation in western Ukraine was targeted (Kyiv Independent).
  • Poland’s defense minister said the missiles fell near Nowy Lipowiec and Rudnik nad Sanem and the drone near Molodycz; one object burned in a field, and no injuries were reported (Kyiv Independent, citing Polish outlets).
  • Poland asked NATO for AWACS and F-35 aircraft; two Gripen fighters and a Dutch tanker supported Polish air defense overnight. Whether the request was granted is not available (Kyiv Independent).
  • NATO Secretary General Rutte called the incident “clearly dangerous” after speaking with Prime Minister Tusk, who convened ministers and security chiefs (Kyiv Independent).
  • Peskov says no dates are set for European officials to visit Moscow; Ryabkov says the invitation does not signal a European role in Ukraine talks (TASS; TASS).

Points of Dispute

Purpose of the strike. Putin calls it retaliation for the 17 Nov Crimean Bridge strike. Ukraine’s response to that framing: not available (Kyiv Independent).

Whether Poland was targeted. Poland’s defense minister said the timing was deliberate, given allied exercises in Poland. Trump said on 19 Nov, before this attack, that Putin has landed drones and “a couple of little missiles, by mistake” in Poland; a White House comment on this incident is not available. Russia’s response is not available (Kyiv Independent).

Watch

  1. Does Poland or another ally request NATO Article 4 consultations over the incident by 05 Dec? Assessment: unlikely (low confidence), based on Poland’s request for aircraft rather than consultations and no Article 4 mention in the reports read.
  2. Does Russia accept a European seat at the next round of Ukraine talks, or direct Europe–Moscow talks? Assessment: unlikely (low confidence), based on Ryabkov’s statement that the invitation signals no European role.

Senate Draft Spending Bills Carry $8.7 Billion in Earmarks as Democrats Signal Stopgap

Draft Senate FY2027 appropriations bills carry $8.7 billion in earmarks requested by 78 senators, and Roll Call reports they could disappear if Congress passes a stopgap after 11 Dec. Democrats have signaled they will seek a stopgap into early 2027.

Change Since Last Report

The 20 Nov report covered Senate Democrats tying any stopgap to Iran war limits. New: draft bill text with earmark detail, and a reported Democratic plan to rewrite the bills in January.

Key Facts

  • The drafts hold 3,956 earmarks in nine of 12 bills; Defense, State-Foreign Operations and Legislative Branch have none. House Republicans’ bills carry nearly 6,700 items worth $8.2 billion (Roll Call).
  • Forty-four Democrats and two independents requested earmarks, as did 32 of 53 Republicans; Democrats and independents received about 64 percent of the projects (Roll Call).
  • McConnell led requests at $405.5 million, followed by Collins at $381 million and Murray at $357 million; the largest single project is a $127.6 million Washington State dam (Roll Call).
  • NPR’s newsletter description says the shutdown deadline next month gives Democrats leverage to block funding for the Iran war (NPR, description only).
  • Absences of House Republicans postponed a vote, and Speaker Johnson urged members to attend (The Hill, headline only).

Points of Dispute

How to fund government past 11 Dec. Democrats, per Roll Call, have signaled a stopgap into the first quarter so they can rewrite the full-year bills when they take the gavels in January; Roll Call adds the rewrite could enlarge the earmark pot or shift it toward blue states. Democrats, per NPR’s description, also see the deadline as leverage on Iran war funding. Republican positions on a stopgap: not available (Roll Call).

Watch

  1. Is a funding bill enacted by 11 Dec without a lapse? Assessment: A lapse is roughly even chance (low confidence), based on the Democratic leverage over Iran funding and the need for at least seven Democratic Senate votes reported 20 Nov.
  2. Does a continuing resolution reach either chamber’s floor by 05 Dec? Assessment: roughly even chance (low confidence), based on the reported Democratic preference for a stopgap and no published Republican position.
