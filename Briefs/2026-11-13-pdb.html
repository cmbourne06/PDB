Trump Won’t Seek Iran Deal; Revolutionary Guard Claims Strike on US Radar in Bahrain

Trump said on 12 Nov he will not seek a deal to formally end the nine-month Iran war and reassigned Vice President Vance, his lead negotiator. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard separately claimed a strike on a US radar in Bahrain; Washington and Manama gave no immediate comment.

Change Since Last Report

The 12 Nov edition reported Iran’s seven conditions sent through Qatar. Trump has since said no deal is needed (W1.8 assessment unchanged), and the Bahrain claim is new.

Key Facts

  • Vance’s new assignment is crime and corruption in Democratic-run jurisdictions (Roll Call).
  • The Guard said it answered an attack on a telecom tower at Sirik; Israel’s security cabinet has reportedly ordered stepped-up preparations for a possible attack on Iran (Al Jazeera).
  • Trump said he held off strikes at Gulf allies’ request, hours after saying he would not hesitate to strike; CENTCOM says the blockade stays in force (Al Jazeera).
  • Brent crude settled at $95.82, up 3.3 percent, and WTI at $88.74; Brent was about $72 before the war began in late February (Epoch Times; Euronews).

Points of Dispute

Whether the war is over. Trump says, “We don’t need a deal with Iran,” and calls the war militarily over (Roll Call). Iran’s negotiator Ghalibaf says “the ball is now in the American court.” Al Jazeera adds that Trump is ready to return to war if Qatar-backed diplomacy fails (Al Jazeera).

Cause of Republican midterm losses. Vance says, “The reason we lost is the war.” Sen. John Kennedy (R-LA) says the war may have contributed but does not explain Texas losses (Daily Beast).

Watch

  1. Is a signed US–Iran deal announced by 30 Nov? Assessment: A signed deal by 30 Nov is very unlikely (moderate confidence), unchanged, based on Trump saying no deal is needed and no reported US reply to Iran’s conditions.
  2. Does CENTCOM or Bahrain publicly confirm damage from the claimed radar strike by 20 Nov? Assessment: Confirmation is unlikely (low confidence), based on no US or Bahraini comment so far.
  3. Does Iran claim another attack on US forces or assets in a Gulf state by 30 Nov? Assessment: Another claim is unlikely (low confidence), based on Reuters’s report that this was the first since early September.

Existing-Home Sales Hit Slowest Pace Since 2010; Freddie Mac Rate Falls a Fourth Week

The National Association of Realtors reports existing-home sales fell 5.5 percent in October, to their lowest level since August 2010. Freddie Mac’s weekly survey put the 30-year mortgage rate at 7.11 percent on 12 Nov, down from 7.18 percent.

Change Since Last Report

The 12 Nov report cited CNBC’s headline of a mortgage-rate jump; Freddie Mac’s survey instead fell. W3.4 resolves yes, against its very unlikely assessment.

Key Facts

  • Sales ran at a 3.75 million annual pace, down 8.8 percent from a year earlier; the median price was $416,700, up 0.4 percent (NAR figures, via HousingWire and Breitbart).
  • Inventory was 1.59 million homes, a 5.1-month supply; first-time buyers were 29 percent of sales and all-cash buyers 29 percent (HousingWire).
  • NAR’s Lawrence Yun tied the decline to mortgage rates near their highest since 2000; Freddie Mac’s October average was 7.33 percent, up from 6.86 percent in September (NAR; Breitbart).
  • Freddie Mac’s 15-year rate was 6.45 percent, down from 6.57 percent the prior week (Freddie Mac).

Points of Dispute

Direction of mortgage rates. CNBC’s 11 Nov headline says rates jumped after four weeks of easing, without identifying the series (CNBC). Freddie Mac’s weekly survey shows no jump (Freddie Mac).

Watch

  1. Does the 08–09 Dec FOMC raise rates? October PPI, due 13 Nov at 08:30 ET, is the next inflation input (BLS). Assessment: A hike is very likely (moderate confidence), unchanged, based on futures pricing of about 29 basis points of hikes by December (market-implied, not Federal Reserve).
  2. Does the December FOMC raise the policy rate by 50 basis points rather than 25? Assessment: A 50-basis-point hike is unlikely (low confidence), unchanged, based on pricing that leaves a half-point move a minority outcome.
  3. Does Freddie Mac’s 30-year rate fall a fifth week in the 19 Nov survey? Assessment: A fifth decline is unlikely (low confidence), based on the 10-year Treasury yield rising about 12 basis points to 5.105 percent on 12 Nov, its highest since 21 Oct (Epoch Times).

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns; CFO Colette Kress Becomes Interim CEO

Nvidia said in an SEC filing on 12 Nov that Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective immediately; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and the filing gives no reason. It follows a board inquiry announced 08 Nov and reports the board was preparing to remove him.

Change Since Last Report

The 12 Nov report relayed press accounts of a planned removal. Nvidia’s filing confirms a resignation; W4.8 and W4.7 resolve (role change; congressional requests, see On the Rise). Inquiry findings: not available.

Key Facts

  • Kress remains CFO while serving as interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO Harvey C. Jones becomes Nvidia’s first board chair (Nvidia Form 8-K).
  • Nvidia shares closed down 4.3 percent on 12 Nov after falling more than 10 percent premarket, while AMD rose 5.9 percent (Epoch Times; Euronews).
  • Reports tie Huang to a Megaspeed executive, whom US intelligence reportedly suspects of spying for China, and to a second relationship with a subordinate (Breitbart; Daily Beast).
  • The New York Times reported, per the Daily Beast, that Trump urged Huang on 10 Nov not to step down; Breitbart says Trump expects investigators to find nothing criminal.

Points of Dispute

Resignation or removal. Nvidia’s filing, the Daily Beast and an NBC News headline describe a resignation (NBC News); Breitbart says the board removed him. The filing gives no reason.

Spy allegation. Breitbart and the Daily Beast relay claims from unnamed officials. Nvidia said on 08 Nov it had no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised (Nvidia).

Watch

  1. Do the Justice Department or FBI publicly confirm an investigation by 31 Dec? Breitbart reports the FBI is examining the relationship (Breitbart). Assessment: Confirmation is roughly even chance (low confidence), unchanged, based on no agency statement in the pages reviewed.
  2. Do Huang or Commerce officials appear before Congress, or does a committee issue a subpoena, by 03 Dec? (Roll Call) Assessment: An appearance by Huang by 03 Dec is roughly even chance (low confidence), based on conflicting reports on whether he agreed to appear.
  3. Does Nvidia name a permanent CEO by 31 Dec? Third-quarter results are due 17 Nov. Assessment: A permanent CEO by 31 Dec is unlikely (low confidence), based on the filing naming an interim CEO.
