About

The Priority Daily Brief is a daily open-source intelligence brief covering the four stories most widely reported across the spectrum in the preceding 24 hours — national security, politics, the economy, and technology.

Every edition is sourced from a curated registry of outlets rated for editorial bias and factual reliability by independent assessors. Sources are selected for balance, not to confirm any particular view.

Indications & Warning

Editions marked ⚠ I&W include up to three items flagged for major significance — developments that may indicate an impending large-scale event or a sudden shift in a key situation.
